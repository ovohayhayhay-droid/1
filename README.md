## Connect with local resources (MEP Center and SBDC)

Manufacturers and small businesses can accelerate planning and execution by working with local support organizations. Prioritize direct outreach to the nearest Manufacturing Extension Partnership (MEP) Center and Small Business Development Center (SBDC), then expand into related regional partners.

## Local resource engagement plan

- Contact the nearest MEP Center for hands-on support with operations, process improvement, technology adoption, and domestic production planning.
- Contact the nearest SBDC for business advising on market strategy, financial planning, and funding readiness.
- Ask both organizations for referrals to local workforce boards, community colleges, chambers of commerce, and procurement assistance providers.
- Schedule recurring check-ins to track milestones, remove blockers, and keep support aligned to current business needs.

## Immediate next steps

1. Identify your county/state MEP Center and SBDC offices.
2. Prepare a one-page business profile with current challenges and 6–12 month goals.
3. Book introductory sessions with both organizations in the next two weeks.
4. Convert recommendations into a simple action tracker with owners and deadlines.

## How to verify

- Confirm the README explicitly references both MEP Center and SBDC.
- Confirm the plan includes concrete outreach actions and follow-up cadence.
- Confirm the next steps are actionable and time-bounded.
